## i) Analysis
I chose these two packets because I wanted to know what do they actually mean and what are inside of them. 

<img width="949" height="32" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3f6f78d2-378d-41ff-b9a2-4d72ff0b8c9f" />

In both packets there is first the details of the packet, destination and source MAC and IP addresses, the source and destination ports etc.. What I was really interested in was the TLS. On the packet 32 the tls has the certificates of the server you try to connect to. A certificate is like an ID and they proof that the server is really the one you are trying to reach.
And on the packet 33 there is the pubkey of the server and on packet 36 is the client pubkey (your pubkey) that are used in the making of the session key that encrypts and decrypts data during the connection.
