# a) WebSDR

Frequency I listened in on was 1070.00 kHz and it was a Los Angeles AM station, and the show which was running on it at time I listened in was "Workers Rights Radio Show". 
The wavelength was not mentioned in the details of the frequency so I put the screenshot to Claude and prompted it to count it for me and the result was 280 meters.
Used AM modulation.
