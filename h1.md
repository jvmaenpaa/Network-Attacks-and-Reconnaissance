# c) ADS-B

I chose the aircraft AFR026 which is owned by Air France. It took of from Los Angeles International Airport 07:53. More details in the picture below. 
<img width="1439" height="911" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1822a16a-6ada-4fa1-b612-6f0f75089ad9" />

The information of the plane is collected with automatic dependent surveillance (ADS) contract requests which are made by a ground system. The reports sent back are sent automatically by the system on board the plane, and the data in these reports is determined by the contract/contracts between the aircraft and the ground system. The contract also determines when, and in which conditions the reports are to be sent.
